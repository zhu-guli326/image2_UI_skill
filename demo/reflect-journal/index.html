Morning Stillness

Today felt slower in the best possible way. I woke up earlier than usual and spent a few quiet minutes watching the light move across the room.

There wasn't any pressure to rush or immediately check my phone. Everything felt softer, calmer, and a little easier to breathe through.

I've been thinking a lot about the resistance I feel before the day begins. This morning made room for a gentler start.
